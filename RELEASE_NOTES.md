# Matcha 1.5.5

A quiet, green keep awake timer for builds, exports, and AI coding agents.

### Changes
- New app icon: a bolder cup of matcha on a deep green tile that stands out in the Dock and Finder.
- New menu bar icon: a solid cup that is easy to see when idle, and turns green with steam while Matcha is keeping your Mac awake.
- New installer window: double click Matcha to be asked whether to move it into Applications, or drag it across yourself. The disk image says what Matcha does and what to do.
- When opened from the disk image or Downloads, Matcha offers once to move itself into Applications and ejects the disk image afterwards.
- The README now leads with a clear download button, step by step install, screenshots, and a short FAQ.

_Install and uninstall instructions live in the [README](https://github.com/jakeantelis/Matcha#install)._
